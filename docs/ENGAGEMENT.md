# Learner streaks and interaction sounds

A streak day is earned when a student earns points for a previously unrewarded correct question (+20) or watches at least 80% of a previously unrewarded video (+10). Existing watch tracking counts unique playback, excluding seeking and looping. Opening lessons, failed answers, duplicate submissions and historical reward backfill do not qualify. Repeating already rewarded work does not qualify on later days either. Teachers and parents do not earn learner streaks.

The home and progress screens show the current and best streak. Yesterday's run remains active until the end of today; a missed calendar day resets the current run but retains the best. Calendar dates use the learner's IANA timezone captured when the engagement record is first saved. Travel does not switch that timezone. Calculations use calendar dates rather than 24-hour durations, including DST, leap days and year boundaries. An open app refreshes the displayed date every 30 seconds and on foregrounding. Future dates from a changed device clock do not extend today's run. This is encouragement based on the device clock, not tamper-proof assessment.

`espada.engagement.v2:<encoded learner ID>` stores points and qualifying dates together in AsyncStorage. The existing v1 points ledger migrates without inventing historical streak dates. Saves are serialized, failed writes are visible and retryable, and actions made during loading are held until the ledger is available. Account changes remount the provider. Storage is local to this browser/device, as with existing rewards; clearing it removes streaks. This does not introduce server sync or multi-tab conflict resolution.

## Sound design and controls

All sounds are original synthesized PCM WAVs generated by `python3 scripts/generate-sounds.py`; no third-party samples or licensed recordings are used. The new cues use a related soft harmonic palette with distinct contours:

- Radar: a 610 ms rising D/G/A sweep, played by the accessible “Оживить карту” action on the actual results radar.
- Points: a 430 ms bright three-note collection cue.
- First qualifying day: a 590 ms warmer, resolving chord.
- Existing navigation, success and level-up cues remain; level-up takes priority over a same-action streak or collection cue. The first-day cue also serves as the points acknowledgment for that action.

The header keeps its mute toggle. Profile adds quiet (20%), moderate (45%), louder (70%) presets and a points preview. Preferences migrate the old mute setting and persist in `espada.sounds.v2`; a failed save shows a retry. No sound plays merely because a screen mounts or saved results load.

Web Audio is created on an interaction, respects browser autoplay restrictions, and drops playback when decoding/resuming fails. Mute, hidden-page transitions, interruption and disposal invalidate pending playback. Native Expo Audio respects silent mode and disables background playback. Higher-priority reward sounds are protected from generic navigation/confetti cues for 850 ms; identical rapid cues are throttled for 180 ms. Reduced motion suppresses radar animation but retains the explicit optional sound action.
